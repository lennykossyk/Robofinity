Robofinity

Modular robotics chassis — Gridfinity floor + DIN rail walls + M3 heat set inserts. Select a part to preview, then download as STL, STEP, or NX.

All connection points are designed for M3 screws and nuts — no special hardware needed.

The Gridfinity Baseplate is compatible with the Gridfinity standard — screw holes + magnet pockets at 32mm pitch.

A browser-based generator (configure size, export STL) is planned. Follow on GitHub.
